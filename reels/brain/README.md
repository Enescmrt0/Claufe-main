## Facts

Einstein died in Princeton on 18 April 1955 and wished to be cremated; his ashes were scattered secretly. Pathologist Thomas Harvey
removed the brain during the autopsy without prior permission, later got reluctant approval from Einstein's son, cut it into about 240
pieces and kept them in jars. He lost his job at Princeton Hospital. Over decades the brain moved with him; journalist Steven Levy found it
in 1978 in jars in a cider box under a beer cooler. Studies (e.g. Diamond 1985) reported unusual features but nothing conclusive. In 1997
Harvey drove across the US with writer Michael Paterniti and the brain in the trunk, visiting Einstein's granddaughter Evelyn. In 1998 he
gave the remaining pieces to the pathologist at Princeton Hospital. Einstein's eye doctor, Henry Abrams, kept his eyes in a New York
safe deposit box.
