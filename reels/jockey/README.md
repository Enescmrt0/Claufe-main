# Frank Hayes: the jockey who won while dead (Belmont Park, 4 June 1923)

Stick-figure reel, old (v1) engine, am_michael voice at 1.12, about 53 s. Props are in `web/env59.js`: galloping racehorse with jump, rider in silks, racetrack with crowd, steeplechase hurdle, stable, odds board, scale, EKG, finish post, gravestone, newspaper.

**Facts used**
- Frank Hayes, a 22-year-old (some sources say 35) stable hand who had never won a race, rode Sweet Kiss in a steeplechase at Belmont Park, New York, on 4 June 1923. The horse's odds are reported as 20 to 1.
- He reportedly crash-dieted to make the weight. He is believed to have suffered a fatal heart attack mid-race, but stayed in the saddle while Sweet Kiss finished first.
- He was found dead when the owner (Miss A. M. Frayling) and officials came to congratulate him. He was buried in his racing silks, and the horse was nicknamed "Sweet Kiss of Death" and reportedly never raced again.
