# Juliane Koepcke: the girl who fell 3 km and lived (24 December 1971)

Stick-figure reel, old (v1) engine, am_michael voice at 1.12, about 57 s. Props are in `web/env60.js`: Lockheed Electra, storm sky and lightning, seat row, rainforest, river, caiman, piranhas, hut and boat, a Peru map (Lima to Pucallpa), and the 92-dot survivor grid.

**Facts used**
- On Christmas Eve 1971, 17-year-old Juliane Koepcke and her mother Maria flew on LANSA Flight 508 (a Lockheed L-188 Electra) from Lima towards Pucallpa.
- The plane flew into a thunderstorm, was struck by lightning and broke up in the air. Juliane fell about 3 km still strapped into her row of seats and woke on the forest floor the next day.
- She had a broken collarbone, a deep cut on her arm, an eye injury and one sandal, and had lost her glasses.
- Following her father's advice, she followed a stream and then a river downstream for about 11 days, past caimans (written as "crocodiles" in the script) and piranhas.
- She found a hut with a boat and an outboard motor. Local loggers found her a few hours later.
- She was the only survivor of 92 people on board (91 died, including her mother). She became a biologist (mammalogist) and runs the Panguana research station in the Peruvian rainforest.
