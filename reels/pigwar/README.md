## Facts

On 15 June 1859, on San Juan Island (claimed by both the US and Britain), American farmer Lyman Cutlar shot a
Hudson's Bay Company pig that was eating his potatoes. He offered $10; the British asked $100 and threatened to arrest
him. Captain George Pickett landed US troops; Britain sent warships. By August, 461 Americans with 14 cannons faced
5 British warships carrying over 2,000 men. Governor James Douglas wanted to land troops; Rear Admiral Robert Baynes
refused to "involve two great nations in a war over a squabble about a pig". Both sides kept a joint occupation for
twelve years without fighting; in 1872 arbitration by German Kaiser Wilhelm I gave the islands to the US. The only
casualty was the pig.
