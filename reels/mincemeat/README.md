## Facts

British intelligence dressed the body of Glyndwr Michael, a homeless Welshman, as the fictional Royal Marines
officer Major William Martin, with "pocket litter" (letters from a fiancée "Pam", theatre ticket stubs, a bank
overdraft letter) and a briefcase of fake letters pointing to Greece and Sardinia instead of Sicily. HMS Seraph put
him in the sea off Huelva, Spain, on 30 April 1943. Copies reached German intelligence and Hitler, who reinforced
Greece. The Allies landed in Sicily in July 1943. Churchill was told "Mincemeat swallowed rod, line and sinker".
In 1998 a line naming Glyndwr Michael was added to the Huelva grave.
