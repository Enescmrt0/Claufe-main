# Roy Sullivan: struck by lightning seven times (1942-1977)

Stick-figure reel, old (v1) engine, am_michael voice at 1.12, about 60 s. Props are in `web/env61.js`: ranger with campaign hat, hair fire, smoke, storm forest, fire lookout tower, park truck, house, ranger station, bucket, pond and fishing rod, bear (from wojtek), 7-bolt tally, trophy, display case with burned hats.

**Facts used**
- Roy C. Sullivan, a US park ranger in Shenandoah National Park, Virginia, was struck by lightning seven times between 1942 and 1977 and survived each one. He holds the Guinness World Record for the most lightning strikes survived.
- 1942: in a fire lookout tower; a burn down his leg and a lost big toenail. 1969: while driving his truck; eyebrows burned off. 1970: in his yard; left shoulder seared. 1972: inside a ranger station; hair set on fire. After this he reportedly kept a pail of water in his truck.
- 1973: hair set on fire again. 1976: an ankle injury. 1977: while fishing, with burns to his chest and stomach; he then reportedly fended off a bear trying to steal his trout by hitting it with a branch.
- People reportedly avoided him during storms. Two of his burned ranger hats are displayed in Guinness World Records exhibits.
