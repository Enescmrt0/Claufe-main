- `web/env19.js`: props: ravens (hop, fly, caw), the White Tower with turrets and flag, curtain wall, a Yeoman Warder

## Facts

At least six ravens are kept at the Tower of London, cared for by the Ravenmaster, a Yeoman Warder. The legend says the
Tower and the kingdom will fall if they leave; the Charles II story is traditional. The ravens get raw meat daily, have
their own lodgings and have flight feathers trimmed; some have still escaped. Tower historian Geoffrey Parnell has
argued the legend is probably a Victorian invention. The last line paraphrases a Ravenmaster's answer.
