## Facts

Pineapples could not be shipped to Britain reliably and had to be grown in heated pineries, taking around two to three
years per fruit, so a single pineapple could cost about as much as a new carriage. They were displayed at dinner
parties as status symbols, and rented out for the evening, then passed on until they spoiled. The Dunmore Pineapple
in Scotland (1761), a garden folly topped by a stone pineapple, still stands.
