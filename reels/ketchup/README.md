## Facts

Early ketchups were made from mushrooms, walnuts, oysters or fish brine. In 1834 Dr. John Cook Bennett claimed
tomatoes could treat diarrhoea, indigestion and other ailments and published tomato-ketchup recipes. Tomato pills
(e.g. Archibald Miles's "extract of tomato") became a craze, but they contained little tomato, and the fad collapsed
by the 1850s.
