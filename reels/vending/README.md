# The 2,000-Year-Old Vending Machine (Hero of Alexandria)

Portrait Short, 1080×1920, 30 fps, about 32 s. Made with the `/stick-figure` skill. Final: `vending-final.mp4`.

- `script.json`: 9 sentences; voice Kokoro `am_michael` at 1.18
- `web/env36.js`: props: Greek temple, Hero's holy-water machine (outside and cut-away with tray, lever and valve),
  bronze coin, Hero of Alexandria, scroll, a modern vending machine that eats a coin
- `audio.py`: SFX (coin drops, pouring water, creaks, clicks, stamps); music `lyre` (lyre and frame drum)

## Facts

Hero of Alexandria (1st century AD) described a coin-operated dispenser for holy water: a coin fell on a pan on a
lever, tipping it to open a valve until the coin slid off and the lever returned, closing the valve.
