## Facts

In the 1960s the CIA's Directorate of Science & Technology ran "Acoustic Kitty", implanting a microphone in a cat's ear canal, a
transmitter at the base of its skull and an antenna woven into its tail, to eavesdrop near Soviet targets. Problems with the cat wandering
off when hungry reportedly led to further surgery. Former CIA official Victor Marchetti said the project cost about $20 million and that
the cat was hit by a taxi on its first test outside the Soviet compound in Washington; other accounts (e.g. Robert Wallace) say the
equipment was removed and the cat lived. A partly declassified 1967 memo concluded the programme would not be practical for the agency's needs.
