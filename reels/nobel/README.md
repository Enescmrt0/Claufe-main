## Facts

When Germany invaded Denmark on 9 April 1940, the Nobel medals of Max von Laue and James Franck were at Niels Bohr's
institute in Copenhagen; exporting gold from Germany was illegal and the names were engraved. George de Hevesy dissolved
the medals in aqua regia and left the solution on a shelf, where it went unnoticed. After the war the gold was
recovered and the Nobel Foundation recast the medals, which were presented again in 1952. Hevesy received the 1943
Nobel Prize in Chemistry.
