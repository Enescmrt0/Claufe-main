## Facts

On 12 November 1970 a dead sperm whale (about 14 m, 8 tons) on the beach at Florence, Oregon, was handled by the
Oregon State Highway Division, because Oregon's beaches were legally highways. They used half a ton of dynamite,
expecting the pieces to be small enough for seagulls. Chunks of blubber flew far over the crowd; one crushed the roof
of a new car. The gulls were scared off and most of the whale stayed on the beach. Florence opened Exploding Whale
Memorial Park in 2020.
