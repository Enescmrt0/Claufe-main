## Facts

Construction began in 1173 and the tower leaned before it was finished (soft clay under the south side). It was closed
in 1990; earlier fixes included concrete and lead counterweights, and a 1995 ground-freezing attempt made it lurch.
Engineers anchored it with steel cables and extracted soil from under the north (high) side; by 2001 about 70 tonnes
(~38 m³) had been removed and the lean was reduced by about 40 cm. It has straightened a few more centimetres since.
