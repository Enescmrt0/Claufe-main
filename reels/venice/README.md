## Facts

Venice's buildings stand on wooden piles (oak, larch, alder) driven into the lagoon's mud and topped with stone. The
Salute church is often said to rest on over a million piles (the figure is widely repeated but weakly sourced, so the
script says "is said to"). Wood-decaying fungi and most bacteria need oxygen, which is almost absent in the deep mud,
so the submerged piles survive for centuries.
