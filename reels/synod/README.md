# The Cadaver Synod (Rome, 897)

Stick-figure reel, old (v1) engine, am_michael voice at 1.12, about 50 s.

**Facts used**
- In January 897, Pope Stephen VI put the exhumed corpse of Pope Formosus (died April 896, about 9 months earlier) on trial in the Lateran basilica.
- The body was dressed in papal vestments and seated on a throne. A deacon answered for it.
- Formosus was found guilty. His acts and ordinations were annulled, the three fingers of his right hand used for blessings were cut off, and the body was thrown into the Tiber.
- The Lateran basilica collapsed soon after, which was taken as a sign. Stephen VI was deposed, imprisoned and strangled in August 897.
- A monk recovered the body. Theodore II had it reburied in St Peter's; John IX annulled the synod and banned trials of the dead.
