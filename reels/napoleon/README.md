## Facts

Napoleon measured about 1.69 m (5 ft 7 in), average or above for a Frenchman of his era. He was recorded as 5 pieds
2 pouces in French units (a French inch was about 2.71 cm, an English inch 2.54 cm). British caricaturists such as
James Gillray drew him as tiny; "le petit caporal" was an affectionate nickname; his Imperial Guard had height minimums.
