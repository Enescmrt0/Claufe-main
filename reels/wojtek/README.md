# Wojtek, the Soldier Bear (1942-1963)

Stick-figure reel, old (v1) engine, am_michael voice at 1.12, about 51 s. Props are in `web/env55.js`.

**Facts used**
- In 1942, Polish soldiers of Anders' Army bought a Syrian brown bear cub from a boy in Iran and named him Wojtek ("smiling warrior").
- He was fed condensed milk from an old vodka bottle and later drank beer. He wrestled the soldiers and ate cigarettes.
- In 1944 the unit sailed from Egypt to Italy and animals were not allowed aboard, so Wojtek was officially enlisted as a private in the 22nd Artillery Supply Company (Polish II Corps).
- At Monte Cassino (1944), according to the soldiers, he carried artillery ammunition crates. This is a soldiers' account, hedged in the script.
- The company's emblem became a bear carrying an artillery shell. He was later promoted to corporal.
- After the war he lived at Edinburgh Zoo until his death in 1963. Polish veterans visited him and spoke to him in Polish, and some climbed in to wrestle him.
