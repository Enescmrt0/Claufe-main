## Facts

In 1957 Alfred Fielding and Marc Chavannes sealed two shower curtains together to make textured wallpaper; it did not
sell, nor did greenhouse insulation. In 1960 their company, Sealed Air, sold it to IBM to protect the new IBM 1401
computer in shipping, and it became a standard packing material.
