## Facts

During WWII, Coca-Cola GmbH in Germany could no longer import syrup ingredients. Max Keith, who ran the German
operation, made a new drink from available by-products, including whey and apple pomace. He asked staff to use their
imagination ("Fantasie"); salesman Joe Knipp suggested "Fanta". Around three million cases were sold in 1943. The
orange-flavoured Fanta was launched in Naples, Italy, in 1955.
