# A grubu: "5 Seconds Without…" metinleri (taslak)

`/stick-figure` formatı: kanca → yaygın beklenti → "But" dönüşü → büyüyen örnekler → kancaya cevap veren kapanış.
Her metin 90-110 kelime (yaklaşık 30-35 sn). Her satır `script.json` dosyasındaki bir cümledir.

Durum: **taslak**. Rakamlar hafızadan yazıldı ve elle hesaplandı; yayından önce kaynaklardan doğrulanmalı.
Her metnin altındaki "Dayanak" bölümü hesabı, "Doğrulanacak" bölümü emin olunmayan yerleri gösterir.

---

## 1. What if gravity switched off for five seconds?

1. What if gravity switched off for five seconds?
2. You'd think everyone would float up into the sky.
3. But nothing would push you upward. You'd just keep whatever motion you already had.
4. Standing still? You'd barely drift.
5. Caught mid-jump? You'd keep rising, about three meters every second.
6. Cars would lose their grip and glide straight off the curves.
7. The air isn't held down anymore, so it starts spreading out toward space.
8. Then gravity snaps back on.
9. And everything that drifted up comes straight back down. From fifteen meters, that's a five-story fall.

- **Dayanak:** Yerçekimi olmayınca cisimler mevcut hızlarını korur (Newton'un birinci yasası). Zıplama hızı yaklaşık 3 m/s; 5 saniyede 15 m. Ağırlık olmayınca lastik tutuşu da kalmaz.
- **Doğrulanacak:** Havanın 5 saniyede ne kadar genişleyeceği. Metin yalnızca "yayılmaya başlar" diyor.

## 2. What if the Sun vanished for five seconds?

1. What if the Sun vanished for five seconds?
2. You'd expect instant darkness and panic.
3. But for eight minutes and twenty seconds, nothing would happen at all.
4. That's how long the Sun's last light takes to reach us.
5. Even its gravity travels at light speed, so Earth keeps orbiting an empty spot.
6. Then the sky goes black. Daytime turns to night, and the stars come out.
7. The Moon goes dark too, because moonlight is just reflected sunlight.
8. Five seconds later, the light is back.
9. Earth drifted off course by less than the width of your hand.
10. The scary part? If the Sun really disappeared, we wouldn't know for eight minutes.

- **Dayanak:** Güneş ışığı Dünya'ya yaklaşık 8 dakika 20 saniyede ulaşır; kütleçekim değişimleri de ışık hızında yayılır. Sapma: ½ × 0,0059 m/s² × 25 s² ≈ 7 cm.
- **Doğrulanacak:** Yok; bunlar yerleşik değerler.

## 3. What if the Earth stopped spinning for five seconds?

1. What if the Earth stopped spinning for five seconds?
2. Sounds harmless. Five seconds is nothing.
3. But at the equator, the ground is moving at almost seventeen hundred kilometers an hour.
4. Stop the planet, and everything on it keeps going.
5. You, your house and the car outside would be thrown east, faster than a jet.
6. The air keeps moving too, a wind stronger than any hurricane ever recorded.
7. The oceans slosh out of their basins as giant waves.
8. The only safe place? The poles, where the ground barely moves at all.
9. Five seconds later the spin returns, to a very different planet.

- **Dayanak:** Ekvatorda dönüş hızı yaklaşık 1.670 km/sa (465 m/s). Yolcu uçakları yaklaşık 900 km/sa hızla uçar.
- **Doğrulanacak:** Yok; ancak senaryo "yalnızca katı zemin durur, üstündeki her şey devam eder" varsayımına dayanıyor.

## 4. What if every sound on Earth vanished for five seconds?

1. What if every sound on Earth vanished for five seconds?
2. You'd think it would feel peaceful.
3. But silence that sudden would set off every alarm in your brain.
4. Your own voice disappears mid-sentence. So does everyone else's.
5. No horns, no sirens, no warning shouts. Drivers and pedestrians lose a whole sense.
6. Bats and dolphins go blind, because they see with echoes.
7. Pilots can't hear the control tower, and the tower can't hear them.
8. Sound is just air shaking. For five seconds, the air stays perfectly still.
9. Then it all crashes back at once, and the world has never sounded so loud.

- **Dayanak:** Ses, ortamdaki titreşimdir. Yarasalar ve yunuslar yankıyla yön bulur.
- **Doğrulanacak:** 3. cümle (ani sessizliğin beyinde alarm tepkisi yaratması) yorum; kaynakla desteklenmeli ya da yumuşatılmalı.

## 5. What if electricity vanished for five seconds?

1. What if electricity vanished for five seconds?
2. You'd picture the lights going out. That's the easy part.
3. Every screen, traffic light and elevator on Earth would freeze.
4. Modern cars would stall, because their engines need a spark.
5. Many modern planes would keep gliding, but nobody could steer them.
6. But here's the real problem. You run on electricity too.
7. Every heartbeat starts with an electrical pulse. Every thought is one.
8. Five seconds without it, and your heart and brain simply pause.
9. Then the power comes back, and eight billion hearts have to start again.

- **Dayanak:** Kalp atışı ve sinir iletimi elektriksel sinyallerle çalışır. Benzinli motorlar buji kıvılcımına ihtiyaç duyar.
- **Doğrulanacak:** 5. cümle (uçakların elektriksiz yönlendirilememesi) uçak tipine göre değişir. 8-9. cümleler spekülasyon; "düşünce deneyi" olarak sunulmalı.

## 6. What if your skeleton vanished for five seconds?

1. What if your skeleton vanished for five seconds?
2. You'd imagine turning into a floppy puddle. Pretty much.
3. Muscles pull on bones to move you. With nothing to pull on, you'd collapse on the spot.
4. But the bigger problem is breathing. Your lungs need the rib cage to expand.
5. Your brain loses its helmet, and your heart loses its cage.
6. You'd even go deaf, because the three smallest bones in your body sit inside your ears.
7. One thing would stay: your teeth. They aren't bones.
8. Then, after five seconds, two hundred and six bones snap back into place.
9. Hopefully the right place.

- **Dayanak:** Yetişkinde 206 kemik vardır. Orta kulaktaki üç kemikçik vücudun en küçük kemikleridir. Dişler kemik sayılmaz.
- **Doğrulanacak:** Yok; bunlar temel anatomi bilgileri.

## 7. What if the Moon vanished for five seconds?

1. What if the Moon vanished for five seconds?
2. Honestly? You'd barely notice.
3. The night sky would blink dark, and that's about it.
4. The tides wouldn't even react. Oceans take hours to respond to the Moon's pull.
5. But if it stayed gone, things get serious.
6. Tides would shrink to about a third, driven only by the Sun.
7. Nights would be far darker, confusing animals that hunt and navigate by moonlight.
8. And over millions of years, Earth's tilt could start to wobble, scrambling the seasons.
9. So the Moon isn't just decoration. It's our planet's stabilizer.

- **Dayanak:** Güneş'in gelgit etkisi Ay'ınkinin yaklaşık yarısı kadardır; Ay olmadan gelgitler bugünkünün kabaca üçte birine iner.
- **Doğrulanacak:** 8. cümle (eksen eğikliğinin kararsızlaşması) bilimsel modellere dayanır ama tartışmalıdır; "could" ile yumuşatıldı.

## 8. What if the entire internet vanished for five seconds?

1. What if the entire internet vanished for five seconds?
2. Five seconds. You'd think nobody would even notice.
3. But in that time, millions of emails and tens of thousands of searches would go nowhere.
4. Every video call freezes. Every online game drops.
5. Card payments fail mid-tap at checkouts around the world.
6. Trading systems that make thousands of deals a second go blind.
7. But the real trouble starts when it comes back.
8. Billions of devices try to reconnect at the exact same moment.
9. Engineers call it a thundering herd, and it can knock servers over for hours.
10. So five seconds of silence could become a very long afternoon.

- **Dayanak:** "Thundering herd", çok sayıda istemcinin aynı anda yeniden bağlanmasıyla oluşan gerçek bir mühendislik sorunudur.
- **Doğrulanacak:** 3. cümledeki büyüklükler (saniyede milyonlarca e-posta, on binlerce arama) yaygın tahminlerdir; güncel kaynakla doğrulanmalı.

## 9. What if air resistance vanished for five seconds?

1. What if air resistance vanished for five seconds?
2. Sounds great. Cyclists and cars would suddenly fly along for free.
3. But air resistance is also the planet's brakes.
4. A feather would fall as fast as a hammer.
5. Raindrops normally top out at around thirty kilometers an hour. Without drag, they just keep accelerating.
6. Skydivers would gain about a hundred and seventy-five kilometers an hour in five seconds.
7. And every open parachute would stop working, because a parachute is nothing but drag.
8. Five seconds later the air pushes back, and everything moving too fast hits a wall of wind.
9. Drag is annoying. It's also why rain doesn't hurt.

- **Dayanak:** Büyük yağmur damlalarının son hızı yaklaşık 9 m/s (32 km/sa). Serbest düşüşte 5 saniyede hız artışı 9,8 × 5 = 49 m/s (176 km/sa).
- **Doğrulanacak:** Yok; ancak "hava var ama direnç yok" durumu fiziksel olarak mümkün değildir, bu bir düşünce deneyidir.

## 10. What if everyone on Earth jumped at the same time?

1. What if everyone on Earth jumped at the same time?
2. People imagine it knocking the planet out of orbit.
3. But all eight billion of us together weigh almost nothing next to the Earth.
4. The planet is about ten trillion times heavier.
5. Packed shoulder to shoulder, we'd all fit inside a single big city.
6. We jump. The Earth moves away by less than the width of an atom.
7. And when we land, it moves right back.
8. The landing would shake the ground nearby and make a deafening thump.
9. The real disaster comes next: eight billion people, all trying to get home from the same place.

- **Dayanak:** İnsanlığın toplam kütlesi yaklaşık 4-5 × 10¹¹ kg, Dünya'nın kütlesi 6 × 10²⁴ kg; oran yaklaşık 10¹³. 8 milyar kişi × 0,25 m² ≈ 2.000 km².
- **Doğrulanacak:** 8. cümledeki sarsıntı ve sesin büyüklüğü; metin rakam vermiyor.
