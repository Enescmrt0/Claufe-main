## Facts

Desmond Doss, a Seventh-day Adventist, served as an Army medic and refused to carry a weapon; in training he was mocked and his superiors
tried to have him discharged. On Okinawa in 1945, after US troops were driven off the Maeda Escarpment ("Hacksaw Ridge"), he stayed on
top under fire and lowered wounded men down the cliff with a rope, praying "Lord, please help me get one more"; the Army estimated he saved
about 75 men. Weeks later he was wounded by a grenade and a sniper's bullet broke his arm; he gave up his place on a stretcher to another
wounded soldier. On 12 October 1945 President Truman presented him the Medal of Honor, the first awarded to a conscientious objector.
