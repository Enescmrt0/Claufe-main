# Tsutomu Yamaguchi: the man who survived both atomic bombs

Stick-figure reel, old (v1) engine, am_michael voice at 1.12, about 48 s. The tone is serious, with elegy music and no comic sound effects. Props are in `web/env57.js`.

**Facts used**
- Tsutomu Yamaguchi (1916-2010), a Mitsubishi naval engineer, was in Hiroshima on a business trip on 6 August 1945, his last day there.
- He was about 3 km from ground zero. The blast ruptured his eardrums and burned his upper body. He spent the night in an air-raid shelter and returned to his hometown, Nagasaki, by train the next day.
- On 9 August, bandaged, he went back to work. While he was describing the Hiroshima blast, his supervisor said one bomb could not destroy a whole city. At that moment the Nagasaki bomb exploded, again about 3 km away.
- He was officially recognised as a survivor of both bombings in 2009, died in 2010 at 93, and spoke out against nuclear weapons in his later years.
