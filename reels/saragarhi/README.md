## Facts

- On 12 September 1897, 21 soldiers of the 36th Sikhs, led by Havildar Ishar Singh, held the Saragarhi signalling post.
  The post sat between Fort Lockhart and Fort Gulistan in the Samana range.
- They faced an estimated 10,000 Afridi and Orakzai tribesmen.
- Signalman Gurmukh Singh reported the battle to Fort Lockhart by heliograph. A relief attempt could not get through.
- The defenders held for hours. The attackers broke through a wall. Gurmukh Singh's last message asked permission to
  take up his rifle.
- All 21 died. Each was posthumously awarded the Indian Order of Merit, the highest gallantry award then open to Indian
  soldiers. Indian soldiers only became eligible for the Victoria Cross in 1911.
- The Sikh Regiment commemorates Saragarhi Day every 12 September.
