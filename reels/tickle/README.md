## Facts

Blakemore, Wolpert and Frith (UCL, late 1990s): the brain uses a copy of its own motor command to predict and dampen
self-produced touch. With a robot that tickled the palm, self-produced strokes felt less ticklish, and adding a delay
of about 100–300 ms between the movement and the touch made them feel more ticklish.
