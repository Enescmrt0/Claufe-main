## Facts

Kutol Products of Cincinnati sold a putty for cleaning coal soot off wallpaper. As homes moved to cleaner heating,
sales collapsed. Nursery-school teacher Kay Zufall saw children modelling with it and told her brother-in-law
Joe McVicker; the company removed the detergent, added colour and, at Zufall's suggestion, named it Play-Doh (1956).
More than three billion cans have been sold.
