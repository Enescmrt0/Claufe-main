# Doomsday: Friday, 13 November 2026 (von Foerster, 1960)

Portrait Short, 1080×1920, 30 fps, about 65 s. Made with the `/stick-figure` skill. Final: `doomsday-final.mp4`.
Timely hook: the predicted date passed twelve days before this was made (made 25 Nov 2026).

- `script.json`: 14 sentences; voice Kokoro `am_michael` at 1.2, word timings from Parakeet
- `web/shots.js`: 14 shots keyed to sentences and words
- `web/env24.js`: props: calendar page, a 1960 journal, population charts (hyperbolic curve to infinity, and a curve
  that levels off), growth-rate hump, a globe crowded with people, asteroid, Heinz von Foerster, warning sign,
  families, phone with a scrolling feed
- `audio.py`: SFX (stamps, alarms, crowd murmur, party horn, swipes); music `clockwork` (tick-tock, bass ostinato,
  staccato strings) turning calm after the warning

## Facts

Heinz von Foerster, Patricia Mora and Lawrence Amiot, "Doomsday: Friday, 13 November, A.D. 2026", Science, 1960. They
fitted about two millennia of population estimates to a faster-than-exponential curve that reached infinity on that
date, von Foerster's 115th birthday (born 13 November 1911). The world's population growth rate peaked in the 1960s
and has fallen since; world population is about 8.2 billion, and UN projections show a peak later this century.
