## Facts

On 10 September 1945 Lloyd Olsen of Fruita, Colorado, beheaded a chicken that kept walking, pecking and preening. The
axe missed the jugular, a clot stopped the bleeding, and most of the brain stem (which controls heart rate, breathing
and reflexes) was left intact. Olsen fed him milk, water and grain with an eyedropper. Mike toured sideshows, featured
in Life magazine and earned up to about $4,500 a month; the University of Utah examined him. He choked in a Phoenix
motel in March 1947 after the syringe used to clear his airway had been left at the previous show. Fruita has held
the Mike the Headless Chicken Festival since 1999.
