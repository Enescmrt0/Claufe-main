## Facts

Violet Jessop was a stewardess on RMS Olympic when it collided with the cruiser HMS Hawke on 20 September 1911; the Olympic returned to
port. In April 1912 she served on Titanic; she was ordered into lifeboat 16 and given a baby to look after, and on the Carpathia a woman
took the baby without a word. In 1916 she served as a nurse with the British Red Cross on the hospital ship HMHS Britannic, which struck a
mine in the Aegean on 21 November 1916 and sank in about 55 minutes. She went back for her toothbrush, jumped from a lifeboat being drawn
toward the propellers, was pulled under and struck her head; years later doctors found she had fractured her skull. She kept working at
sea for decades and became known as "Miss Unsinkable".
