## Facts

Returning from his Grand Embassy in 1698, Peter the Great shaved several nobles himself at a reception and introduced
a beard tax; the wealthiest merchants paid up to 100 rubles a year. Payers received a copper beard token
inscribed "the beard is a superfluous burden". The tax was abolished in 1772.
