## Facts

In November 1902 Theodore Roosevelt hunted bear in Mississippi; after days without success his guides tied a black
bear to a tree, and he refused to shoot it as unsporting. Clifford Berryman's cartoon "Drawing the Line in
Mississippi" ran in The Washington Post. Morris and Rose Michtom, Brooklyn candy-shop owners, made a stuffed bear and
displayed it as "Teddy's bear"; demand led to the Ideal Novelty and Toy Company.
