## Facts

The 23rd Headquarters Special Troops, about 1,100 men including many artists and designers, impersonated other Allied units with inflatable
tanks, trucks and artillery, sound trucks playing recorded battle noise audible miles away, fake radio traffic, and staged rumours (e.g. in
cafés). In a well-known anecdote, two French cyclists saw four soldiers lift a "40-ton" tank and were told Americans are very strong. The
unit staged more than 20 deceptions; historians estimate it saved thousands of lives. Its work stayed classified until 1996, and in 2022 the
unit was awarded the Congressional Gold Medal.
