## Facts

About 7,300 tonnes of iron and some 2.5 million rivets. Iron expands about 12 µm per metre per °C, so a 1 m bar
heated by 30 °C grows about 0.36 mm, and 300 m of iron about 11 cm; quoted figures range up to 15 cm. The sunny side
expands more, so the top tilts away from the sun during the day and straightens at night. Eiffel designed the tower
to flex with wind and heat. Opened 1889.
