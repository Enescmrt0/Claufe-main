## Facts

Lt. Hiroo Onoda was sent to Lubang Island in the Philippines in December 1944 with orders never to surrender. He dismissed
leaflets announcing Japan's surrender as propaganda and kept up a guerrilla campaign; his group's raids killed about 30
islanders. His companions surrendered (1950) or were killed (1954, 1972), leaving him alone. In February 1974 Japanese
traveller Norio Suzuki, who said he was looking for "Lieutenant Onoda, a panda and the Abominable Snowman, in that order",
found him within days. Onoda would only stand down on orders from his commander; Suzuki found Maj. Yoshimi Taniguchi, by then
a bookseller, who flew to Lubang on 9 March 1974 and read him the order. Onoda surrendered and handed over his sword.
