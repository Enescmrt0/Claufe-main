## Facts

Hubble launched in 1990, is about 13 m long (school-bus size) and has no propulsion. Five shuttle servicing missions
(1993–2009) repaired it and some raised its orbit; the shuttle retired in 2011. Atmospheric drag lowers its orbit;
NASA projects re-entry around 2033–2034. On 29 October 2026 NASA Goddard issued a non-binding request for information
on reboost, servicing or controlled disposal, with responses due 30 November 2026; any mission would need to fly by
about 2032. NASA reviewed a 2022 commercial reboost proposal and declined in 2024, citing cost and the risk of damage.
