## Facts

Joshua Abraham Norton lost his fortune in the 1850s after trying to corner San Francisco's rice market. On 17 September 1859 he
proclaimed himself "Emperor of these United States" in a letter the San Francisco Bulletin printed. He wore a blue military uniform with
gold epaulettes and a beaver hat with a feather, issued decrees (including ordering Congress dissolved) and his own scrip, which some
businesses honoured; restaurants often fed him free. After a police officer arrested him in 1867, public outcry led the police chief to
release him, and officers afterwards saluted him. The city's Board of Supervisors paid for a new uniform when his wore out. In 1872 he
decreed a bridge between San Francisco and Oakland; the San Francisco-Oakland Bay Bridge opened in 1936. He died in January 1880, and
estimates of the funeral crowd run from 10,000 up to 30,000.
