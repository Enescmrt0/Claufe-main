Science notes: sweet taste is detected by T1R2/T1R3 receptors and engages dopamine reward pathways; gut
"neuropod" cells sense sugar and signal the brain through the vagus nerve, and in mouse studies they distinguish
sugar from non-caloric sweeteners (one proposed reason sweeteners satisfy less). The spike/insulin/crash
sequence is simplified; how strongly someone "crashes" varies, hence "you can crash".
