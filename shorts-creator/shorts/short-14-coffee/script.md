Science notes: withdrawal typically starts 12–24 h after the last dose, peaks around 20–51 h and lasts 2–9 days;
caffeine constricts cerebral blood vessels (rebound dilation drives the headache); caffeine reduces deep (slow-wave) sleep.
Individual timelines vary, hence the on-screen "general info, not medical advice" note.
