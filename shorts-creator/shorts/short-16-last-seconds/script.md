Science notes (why the hedged wording):
- Loss of consciousness within roughly 10–20 s of circulatory arrest is the commonly cited range.
- Gamma surge: seen in rats after cardiac arrest (Borjigin et al., 2013), in a single human EEG case
  (Vicente et al., 2022) and in 2 of 4 dying patients (Xu et al., 2023). Small samples, so "in some recordings".
- The link to "life flashing before your eyes" is a hypothesis, labelled on screen as not proven.
- Hearing: EEG responses to sound in unresponsive hospice patients (Blundon et al., 2020), hence "may be".
- "One final wave": terminal spreading depolarization, recorded minutes after circulatory arrest (Dreier et al., 2018).
