Science notes (why the hedged wording):
- Sweetness: aspartame is roughly 200x and sucralose roughly 600x as sweet as sugar by weight.
- Weaker reward response and the cravings question come from imaging and appetite studies with mixed results,
  so the video says "suggest", "some studies" and shows "EVIDENCE: MIXED".
- The memory link is from observational cohort studies; the on-screen stamp says "CORRELATION ≠ PROOF".
  The scatter chart is an illustration, not real data.
- Food-safety regulators set an acceptable daily intake for approved sweeteners.
